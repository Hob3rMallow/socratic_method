# Paper and release figures

The LaTeX paper detects these files automatically (PDF preferred, PNG accepted),
scales them into their reserved panels, and otherwise leaves a labeled box:

1. `teaser` - a two-row blind PHerc1447 de-blob and locked PHerc0139 growth
   comparison from the same raw 8,192-sample student at threshold 0.45, with
   released M7 shown in both rows.
2. `figure_1_method_overview` - released M7 state-of-the-art baseline, fine
   teacher, projection, student losses, trust region, raw inference, and
   postprocess boundary.
3. `figure_crossres_supervision` - fine-teacher mask, LSMAT-style medial
   centers with slice-wise radii, soft occupancy and binary crest projections,
   de-blob separation shell, and a dynamic widest-path event with pins, free
   anchors, corridor, and selected bottleneck path.
4. `figure_2_registered_examples` - locked registered examples comparing CT,
   M7, fine-teacher target, selected raw student, and two-sided difference.
5. `figure_3_line_fitter_examples` - measured released-M7 masks before and after
   accepted additions, a connection track across z, registered fine-CT
   connected/separate checks, and the calibrated run-4 statistics.
6. `figure_4_failure_cases` - the rank-26 topology-metric exception, rank-64
   thinning/drift, and remaining blind undergrowth or fragmentation.

Supplemental release sheets generated by the same script are
`locked16_release_grid.png`, `pherc1447_release_grid.png`, and
`figure_duration_selection.png`. The first two are complete side-by-side audit
sheets rather than cherry-picked overlays.

The paper ends with `gallery_locked_growth.png` and
`gallery_blind_antiblob.png`: two full-page, side-by-side qualitative galleries
drawn only from the committed review corpus. Both galleries show released M7;
the blind gallery deliberately excludes PHerc1447 z=12096 and z=12936.

The generated `manifest.json` records every discrete image/JSON input with
SHA-256 and records the identity file for each Zarr source. Colors recover the
original report grammar: released M7 cyan, projected teacher or comparison
reference yellow, raw student neon magenta, shared foreground green, medial
evidence violet, and connectivity pins orange, all on dark navy.
