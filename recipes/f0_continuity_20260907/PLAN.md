# Continued continuity work

Goal: improve usable continuity beyond the local 21-pixel native filler,
without changing F0/200k, its .35 operating threshold, or the archived model.

The initial development-only probes inspect curved paths on skeletons of the
cached probability field. They are diagnostic proposals, not accepted masks.
Global low-threshold masks are never substituted for the original foreground.

Completion requires measured larger/curved gap recovery, not merely more paint:

1. Implement probability-supported seeded corridor proposals and protect all
   existing F0/native-filler foreground. No detached low-confidence islands.
2. Test genuine long/bent continuations and negative cases: parallel sheets,
   third-fragment contact, off-ridge wandering, missing evidence and interacting
   repairs. Record all proposal refusals, not just accepted examples.
3. Run the new mechanism across real contiguous development context and inspect
   accepted long/core repairs, broad-field impact, and 3D interacting chains.
4. Freeze the candidate policy before evaluating a fresh control region. The
   earlier control is not treated as fresh independent validation.
5. Produce CT-aligned colored comparisons, quantitative continuity/change
   evidence, explicit failures/limits, and an opt-in checked implementation.
   Do not promote a weak or unverified result into the official profile.

No new training, model blend, global threshold change, destructive mask edits,
public upload, automatic production deployment, or multi-worker inference fleet.
Existing caches suffice for development; at most one bounded GPU inference
worker is needed later for fresh control context.

Important distinction: global umbilicus radius is not constant along folded
local sheets. New curved paths can exceed the native endpoint radial limit;
that must be justified by local evidence and wrong-sheet checks, not advertised
as if the original radial certificate still applies to the new stage.

The first visual probes show promising 28–62 pixel continuations and also
wandering low-probability paths. In particular, CT center brightness is not a
reliable stand-alone gate: a correct prediction can trace a tissue interface
alongside a brighter neighboring peak. Keep CT review, probability ridge
separation, endpoint geometry and real axial evidence distinct.
